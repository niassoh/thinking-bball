# Source Photo Selection

The source image is a major part of the final quality. Do not simply grab the first official headshot.

## Target source-photo energy

Prefer images that feel like a real basketball moment rather than an ID photo. Good expressions include:

- focused or intense gaze
- looking to the side/upcourt
- reacting to a play
- talking or shouting
- celebration
- competitive expression
- natural game-time concentration

The goal is **somewhat emotive or dynamic**, not necessarily the most extreme frame available. Aim for roughly **6–8/10 energy**: visually interesting but still readable as a profile portrait after a tight crop.

A neutral source is acceptable only when no strong, clean game/editorial image is available.

## Technical requirements

Prefer a candidate where:

- the face is sharp and unobstructed
- the head is fully visible
- facial features are not motion-blurred
- the face is at least ~250 px tall in the source
- the head is not at an extreme perspective
- the player is not hidden by a ball, hand, towel, microphone, another player, etc.
- hair is visible and not clipped
- lighting is usable and the face is not crushed into shadow
- the source is reasonably recent
- current hairstyle / facial hair is preferred
- current team uniform is preferred when practical

## Source priority

1. Authorized official team / league photography or CDN image
2. Authorized high-resolution sports/editorial photography
3. Other high-quality game photography you are permitted to use

If you have a player ID and an official headshot endpoint, check it first—but **do not automatically choose it** if a better dynamic game image is available.

## Candidate scoring

When multiple candidates are available, score them and choose the highest total:

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Face clarity / sharpness | 0–3 |
| Face size / cropability | 0–3 |
| Expression / game energy | 0–3 |
| Minimal occlusion | 0–2 |
| Useful head angle / silhouette | 0–2 |
| Recent/current appearance | 0–2 |
| Current jersey/team relevance | 0–1 |

Maximum: 16.

Prefer a slightly lower-resolution image with a much better expression over a sterile official portrait, as long as the face remains large and sharp enough.

## Reject a source if

- the face is tiny
- the player is heavily motion-blurred
- a hand/ball/object covers important facial features
- the head is cut off
- expression is distorted by a bad frame
- heavy compression prevents clean facial interpretation
- lighting makes skin tone impossible to recover reliably

Always retain the selected original file locally for reproducibility.
